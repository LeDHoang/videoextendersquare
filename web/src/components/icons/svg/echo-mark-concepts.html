ECHO mark: concepts

Today's mark has ten parts in a 24-unit box, so at the 30 px header size the eye is about 1.5 px across. C and D are the rounded directions you kept. A and B are new: they keep the old mark's sharp, chamfered, brutalist geometry and build on the name, an eye looking at echoes of worlds. Hover any big mark (or header bar) to play its hover animation, and move the pointer around it: the eye follows you. When idle, every eye still glances and blinks on random timers.
