# Earth texture source

`earth-natural-1024x512.jpg` is a 1024×512 derivative of the Natural Earth II
raster map. Natural Earth data is in the public domain.

- Source: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-raster-data/10m-natural-earth-2/
- License: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
- Local modifications: resized and JPEG-compressed for the WebXR globe.
