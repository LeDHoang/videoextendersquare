# Earth texture source (4K)

`earth-natural-4096x2048.jpg` is a 4096×2048 derivative of the Natural Earth II
with Shaded Relief, Water, and Drainages raster (50m, 10800×5400). Natural Earth
data is in the public domain.

- Source: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/50m-raster-data/50m-natural-earth-2/
- License: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
- Local modifications: resized to 4096×2048 and JPEG-compressed for the WebXR globe.
